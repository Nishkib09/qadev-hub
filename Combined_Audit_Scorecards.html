How to connect your Google Sheet (UPDATED SCRIPT — Action Required)  — Must re-deploy for email & Drive to work

String(d.auditId) : ""; var duplicateAudit = false; var diag = 'Payload: files=' + (d.files ? d.files.length : 0) + ', agentName="' + (d.agentName || 'EMPTY') + '"' + ', emailOnly=' + (d.emailOnly ?
